Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the “Goug” app (the “app”) handles your data. In short: your data stays on your iPhone. The app's publisher has no server, creates no account and receives none of your data.

Data controller

Gregory Frasson — contact: canapes_parloirs.3o@icloud.com

Data stored on your device

The app stores only on your iPhone (and on your Apple Watch if you use it):

  • your profile: sex, age, height, weight, goal, activity level;
  • your tracking: weigh-ins, meals and foods, water, workouts, fasts, body measurements, mood and energy, habits, challenges, recipes, planned meals, treatment journal (doses, dose amount, side effects, if you use it);
  • your progress and meal photos, if you add any (progress photos can be protected by a passcode and encrypted);
  • your settings.

This data is never sent to the publisher or to any third party by the app. It is deleted if you delete the app.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads your weight, workouts, steps, active energy, sleep and, if you turn on cycle tracking, your period dates from Apple Health. It writes your weight, water and meal energy to it, along with each meal's protein, carbs, fat and fiber (setting “Write macros to Health”, which you can turn off). If you turn on “Import my workouts from Health” (off by default), it reads your workouts from the last 30 days, then new ones, and adds them to your sport log on your iPhone; workouts the app recorded itself are not imported a second time. On Apple Watch, during a workout you start, the app reads your heart rate and active calories to show the workout, then saves it to Health and Fitness. You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings ▸ Health.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with third parties, and is not stored in iCloud by the app.

Services used

  • Open Food Facts (collaborative food database, openfoodfacts.org): when you search for a product online or scan a barcode, the app sends the search term or barcode to Open Food Facts. As with any internet connection, your IP address is visible to their server. The app language and your iPhone's region (to rank results) go with it; no other data is sent. See Open Food Facts' privacy policy.
  • Apple Intelligence: the weekly analysis, the coach and photo portion estimates use Apple's model on the device. No data leaves your iPhone.
  • Photo recognition: foods are recognized on the iPhone (Apple Vision), with nothing sent online.

What you choose to share

The app shares nothing by itself. Some actions, triggered only by you, take data out of the app:

  • “Copy” or “Share” on your summary or analysis text: copied text stays on your iPhone (no clipboard shared with your other devices) and is erased after 3 minutes; sharing sends it to the app you choose;
  • exporting your data (JSON file), PDF reports or a progress card, to the location or recipient you choose;
  • sending your shopping list to the Reminders app;
  • automatic backup, to a folder on your iPhone that you choose;
  • sending a crash report (see below).

Crash reports

After a crash, iOS may give the app a technical report (with no personal data). It stays on your iPhone and is only sent if you choose to share it from Settings.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your rights

Because your data is only on your device, you keep full control: you can view, export (Settings ▸ Data), edit or delete it at any time, or delete the app to erase everything. For any question, or to exercise your GDPR rights, write to canapes_parloirs.3o@icloud.com. You may also contact your data protection authority.

Minors

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Health disclaimer

The app provides general wellness information. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a healthcare professional. See a doctor before starting a diet or a fast, especially if you are pregnant, diabetic or on medication.

Changes

This policy may change; the update date is shown at the top of this page.
